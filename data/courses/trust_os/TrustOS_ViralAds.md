## PRODUCTION NOTES
- TALENT: Real people, not models. Ages 25–45. Black professionals — authentic, not corporate.
- SETTING: Real environments. Kitchen tables, cars, home offices. Imperfect backgrounds are fine.
- CAMERA: Phone camera or DSLR shot to look like phone camera. Vertical 9:16 format.
- TEXT OVERLAYS: Bold, clean font (Impact or Montserrat Heavy). White text on black bar.
- CAPTIONS ON EVERY AD: 85% of social video is watched on mute.
- COMMENT BAIT: End each ad with engagement trigger: "Tag someone who needs this."
