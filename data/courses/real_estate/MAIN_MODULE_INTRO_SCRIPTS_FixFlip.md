## MODULE 0: Welcome to The Informed Investor™
**Estimated runtime: ~2 minutes**

Real estate is one of the most forgiving wealth-building vehicles available — when you know what you are doing. It is also one of the most expensive classrooms in the world when you do not.

The difference between those two outcomes is knowledge. That is why this course exists.

Welcome to The Informed Investor: Fix and Flip Foundations. Before we get into deal analysis, market data, contractor bids, or exit strategies, there are three things you need to understand — why most investors fail, how an informed investor actually thinks, and how to use AI as a tool throughout everything we cover here. Module Zero is short by design. It is not optional.

In Lesson One, we break down the four real reasons investors lose money on deals — and none of them are bad luck. In Lesson Two, we look at the Informed Investor framework that will run through every module in this course. The framework is the lens. Everything after this module is what you see through it. And in Lesson Three, you will set up your AI workflow so you are ready to use the tools embedded in this course from day one.

Complete this module before you touch anything else. Take five minutes and fill out your Investor Goals Sheet at the end. Write down what you are building toward and why. That clarity becomes fuel when a deal gets complicated.

Everything that follows builds on what we establish here. Get through it, set your foundation, and let's move.
